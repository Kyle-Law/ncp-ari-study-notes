Extra-wide racks for AI platforms

The equipment still mounts on standard 19-inch rails. What an AI rack needs extra room for is everything around it: hundreds of thick cables, several PDUs, liquid manifolds, and space to reach and pull every transceiver.

Exam objectiveDescribe extra-wide racks for AI platforms.

In one minute

  • A standard rack is 600 mm wide. AI and network racks go to 750–800 mm. The 19-inch (EIA-310) mounting opening stays the same, and the extra width becomes side channels for vertical cable management.
  • Schneider's AI guidance (WP110) recommends minimums of 750 mm wide, 1,200 mm deep (mounting depth > 1,000 mm), 48U, and > 2,270 kg static load capacity (> 1,600 kg dynamic).
  • Why: high-radix switches (e.g. 32 twin-port OSFP cages) land dozens of cables per U; copper is ~9.4 mm thick; racks carry multiple zero-U PDUs and liquid manifolds at the rear.
  • Wide racks let cables leave the switch straight to the sides (50/50) without crossing the face, keeping transceivers, fans and PSUs serviceable and air flowing.
  • Some AI racks aren't 19-inch at all: GB200 NVL72 is a 48U OCP/MGX-style rack (~600 × 1,068 mm, ~1,360 kg). Check doors, lifts and floor too.

Why AI racks outgrow 600 mm

Cable density

A 64-port NDR switch with breakouts can land 64+ cables in 1U. With copper at ~9.4 mm, a 600 mm rack has nowhere to route them except across the face.

Serviceability

NVIDIA's best practices: choose a rack wide enough that cables pass between the switch and the side walls, so transceivers and fans stay reachable.

Power & liquid

Multiple high-amp PDUs (A/B, sometimes several per side), plus liquid supply/return manifolds, compete for the rear corners.

Depth

Deep servers (DGX H100 is up to 897 mm) plus rear cable space and PDUs push depth to 1,200 mm.

Weight

Dense AI racks can exceed 1,800 kg, so static and dynamic (rolling) load ratings and leveling feet matter.

Airflow

Cables kept in side channels leave the front and rear faces clear. Pair with perforated doors and containment.

Exam traps

  • "Extra-wide racks need wider equipment."No. Equipment still mounts to the 19-inch EIA-310 opening. The extra width is for cable management and power.
  • Width without depth.AI racks also need ~1,200 mm depth for long servers, rear cabling, PDUs and manifolds.
  • Assuming every AI rack is 19-inch.GB200 NVL72 uses its own 48U rack with a ~600 × 1,068 mm footprint.
  • Forgetting the floor.Weight ratings for the rack and for the floor along the delivery path.

Check yourself

1. What is the extra width of an 800 mm rack mainly used for?

The mounting opening is still 19-inch. The extra width creates side channels for cables (and room for PDUs).

2. WP110's recommended minimum width for AI racks?

750 mm wide, 1,200 mm deep, 48U, > 2,270 kg static capacity.

3. Why does a narrow rack hurt serviceability in a dense switch rack?

Without side space, cables drape over ports and vents.
