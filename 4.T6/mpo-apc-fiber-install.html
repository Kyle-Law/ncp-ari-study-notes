Installing MPO/APC fiber

An MPO connector puts a whole row of fibers in one ferrule. It packs a 400G link into one plug, but every fiber in the row has to be clean, aligned and mapped to the right position at the far end.

Exam objectiveInstall MPO/APC fiber optic cables.

In one minute

  • MPO = multi-fiber push-on (MTP is a high-performance MPO brand). NVIDIA's NDR fiber uses MPO-12: 12 positions, 8 lit (4 Tx + 4 Rx).
  • APC = angled physical contact, polished at 8°, with a green body. The angle sends reflections into the cladding instead of back at the laser, which matters for PAM4. NVIDIA's NDR passive fiber cables (MFP7E…) are MPO-12/APC.
  • Never mate APC with UPC (flat polish). The faces don't meet, so you get high loss, strong reflection and possible end-face damage.
  • Pinned mates unpinned: two guide pins on one side, two holes on the other. Never pin-to-pin or hole-to-hole.
  • Polarity: transmit fibers must land on receive fibers. For parallel optics the end-to-end path must act like a Type B (key-up to key-up) cable.
  • Inspect → clean → inspect both the cable end and the port on every insertion, using an MPO click cleaner and an APC probe tip.

Anatomy of an MPO-12/APC

Polarity: make Tx land on Rx

A parallel optic (SR4, DR4) transmits on positions 1–4 and receives on 9–12. Choose a cable type and watch where each transmit fiber lands at the far transceiver.

Type (TIA-568)KeysMappingParallel optics, end to end?
AKey up → key down1→1 … 12→12No: Tx lands on Tx
BKey up → key up1→12, 2→11 … 12→1Yes
CKey up → key downPairs flipped: 1→2, 2→1 …No (built for duplex breakouts)

With patch panels and trunks in the path, what counts is the net mapping of all components together. For parallel optics it must work out to Type B. Follow the site's polarity method and label both ends.

The install, step by step

  1. Check the plan and the partFiber type (yellow SMF / aqua MMF), APC vs UPC, fiber count (MPO-12), pinned/unpinned ends, polarity type, length. Label both ends before routing.
  2. Route first, plug lastLay the cable with dust caps on. Pull pre-terminated trunks by their pulling grip or the aramid strength members, never by the connector or jacket. Respect the bend radius: about 10× OD at rest, 20× under tension as a rule of thumb (the datasheet wins).
  3. Inspect, clean, inspectClean the cable end-face and the transceiver port with an MPO click cleaner. Inspect with an MPO probe (APC tip for APC), panning across all 12 positions. Repeat until clean.
  4. Mate immediatelyAlign the key, push straight in until it latches. Don't leave a cleaned face exposed. If a connector comes out, even by accident, clean it again.
  5. Dress and supportHook-and-loop ties (never zip ties on fiber), supports every ≤ 2 m, no weight on the connector, clear of vents and transceiver pull-paths.
  6. VerifyPort LED and link state, then optical receive power and error counters (e.g. mlxlink module and BER readings). Low Rx power on one lane usually means a dirty or damaged position.

Exam traps

  • APC into UPC.The angled and flat faces don't meet, giving high loss, high reflection and possible damage. Green mates only with green.
  • Pin to pin.Two pinned ends can't mate and will damage pins. Two unpinned ends mate loosely and misalign.
  • "Clean once at install."NVIDIA's rule is every insertion, both sides, including after an accidental unplug.
  • Wrong probe tip.APC end-faces need an APC tip, and cable plugs need an MPO-to-MPO adapter before the probe can enter.
  • Type A cable between two transceivers.Tx faces Tx and the link stays down, even though all fibers are clean and lit.

Check yourself

1. What does the green body of an MPO connector indicate?

Connector colour indicates polish: green = APC. Jacket colour indicates fiber type.

2. Why does APC matter for NDR links?

The 8° face sends reflected light into the cladding instead of back toward the transmitter.

3. A direct transceiver-to-transceiver MPO link needs which polarity type?

Type B reverses positions (1→12 …) so Tx on 1–4 lands on Rx on 9–12.

4. A cable was unplugged by accident and is going straight back in. What first?

Every insertion gets cleaned, both sides. Canned air scatters particles and leaves oils.

5. How many of the 12 MPO positions does a DR4/SR4 link light?

4 transmit + 4 receive. The middle 4 are unused but still collect dust.
