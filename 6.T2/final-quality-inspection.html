Final deployment quality inspection

Before handover, walk every rack and pathway with a checklist. The point is to catch the problems that don't break a link today but will tomorrow: a too-tight bend, a missing label, a bundle in front of a fan intake.

Exam objectiveConduct final deployment quality inspection (bend radius, labeling, airflow).

In one minute

  • Bend radius: no bend tighter than the datasheet minimum. Rule of thumb ≈ 10× OD installed, 20× under pulling tension. Look hardest at the tight turns: at the port, into managers, at tray drops.
  • Labeling: every cable labelled at both ends, legible, unique, matching the cable schedule / as-built. Racks, panels and ports labelled too (TIA-606).
  • Airflow: nothing blocking intakes, exhausts, fans or PSUs; blanking panels in place; no abandoned cable; rack centre clear (50/50 routing).
  • Plus: supports ≤ 2 m (≤ 60 cm in racks), hook-and-loop only and snug, connectors fully latched with no weight on them, dust caps on unused ports, all links up at the expected speed.
  • Record the result: photos, test reports and a punch list of fixes.

Bend radius check

The minimum radius scales with the cable's outer diameter (OD). Enter a cable to get rule-of-thumb limits, then always confirm against its datasheet.

10× / 20× OD are common rules of thumb (FOA). Bend-insensitive fibers allow tighter radii; thick copper often needs more. The datasheet value wins.

Exam traps

  • "All links are up, so it passes."QA also covers physical workmanship that hasn't failed yet: bends, labels, support, airflow.
  • Label at one end.Both ends, every cable, matching the records.
  • Ignoring the rear.Rear cable bundles in front of exhausts or PSU handles fail an airflow and serviceability check.
  • Zip ties on fiber.An automatic finding. Replace with hook-and-loop.

Check yourself

1. Rule-of-thumb minimum bend radius for an installed 3 mm AOC?

≈ 10× OD installed, 20× while pulling. Always check the datasheet.

2. Which labeling finding fails QA?

Every cable needs a unique label at both ends that matches the documentation.

3. An airflow finding during inspection?

Anything blocking intakes, exhausts, fans or PSUs is a finding. Re-route through the side managers.
