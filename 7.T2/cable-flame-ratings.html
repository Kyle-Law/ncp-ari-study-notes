Fire safety and cable flame ratings

A data hall holds tonnes of cable jacket, and burning jackets spread flame and smoke. The NEC sets how fire-resistant a cable must be by where it runs. The strictest rule applies to air-handling spaces, because smoke there reaches everyone.

Exam objectiveComply with fire safety and cable flame ratings (NEC plenum requirements).

In one minute

  • Copper communications cable ratings, highest first: CMP (plenum) > CMR (riser) > CMG / CM (general) > CMX (limited). Fiber: OFNP > OFNR > OFNG / OFN (N = non-conductive; OFC… = conductive).
  • Plenum = a space used for environmental air (above a drop ceiling or under a raised floor used as an air path). Cables there must be plenum-rated or run in an approved raceway or metal conduit.
  • Riser = vertical runs between floors (shafts). CMR/OFNR or better.
  • Substitution goes down, never up: a CMP cable can be used anywhere; a CMR cable can't go in a plenum.
  • Also: firestop every penetration through a fire-rated wall or floor, remove abandoned cable (NEC), and know local equivalents (e.g. EU CPR Euroclasses, LSZH jackets). IT rooms built to NEC 645 have their own rules for under-floor cabling.

Fire safety beyond the jacket

Firestopping

Every cable penetration through a fire-rated wall or floor is sealed with a listed firestop system, and re-sealed after any change.

Abandoned cable

The NEC requires removal of abandoned cable (not terminated and not tagged for future use). It's fuel load and blocks airflow.

Plenum raceway

Non-plenum cable may cross a plenum only inside approved plenum raceway or metal conduit.

Clearances

Keep trays and bundles clear of sprinkler heads and fire-detection devices so they work as designed.

Outside the US

EU CPR classes (Aca, B1ca, B2ca, Cca, Dca, Eca) and low-smoke zero-halogen (LSZH) jackets are common requirements. Check local codes.

Suppression

Clean-agent or pre-action sprinkler systems are typical in data halls. Coordinate cable pathways with them.

Exam traps

  • CMR in a plenum.Not allowed. Plenum spaces need CMP/OFNP (or approved raceway).
  • Thinking higher-rated is "wrong" for lower spaces.CMP may be used anywhere, including riser and general spaces.
  • Every ceiling is a plenum.Only if it's used for environmental air. A ducted-return ceiling may not be a plenum. Check the HVAC design.
  • Leaving dead cable "just in case".Untagged abandoned cable must be removed.

Check yourself

1. Fiber runs above a drop ceiling that serves as the return-air plenum. Minimum rating?

Air-handling plenums need plenum-rated cable (OFNP for non-conductive fiber) or approved raceway.

2. Can a CMP cable be installed in a riser?

Substitution runs downward: CMP → CMR → CM → CMX.

3. What test is associated with plenum-rated cable?

NFPA 262 measures flame spread and smoke for plenum cables. UL 1666 is the riser test.

4. What does the NEC require for abandoned communications cable?

Abandoned cable adds fuel load and must be removed unless it's tagged for future use.
